# Targeting and verification

## Ideal company profile

A company is eligible when all of these are true:

- It sells primarily to consumers or has a clear consumer-facing brand.
- It operates in Argentina or Spain.
- It has an active need for social, brand, performance, ecommerce, content, or paid-media creative.
- Street-interview video could plausibly support its organic content or ads.
- It is not already represented beyond the allowed daily/contact capacity.

Strong signals include active Instagram/TikTok/YouTube output, visible Meta/Google/TikTok advertising, recent product launches, retail expansion, ecommerce promotions, or recurring creator-led content. Record only signals actually observed.

## Company exclusions

Exclude agencies, direct Charlando competitors, pure B2B services without a consumer brand, dormant businesses, out-of-scope countries, and companies already at their daily/contact limit.

## Role ladder

Prefer, in order:

1. Head/Director/Lead/Manager of Brand, Marketing, Growth, Performance, Paid Media, Content, Social Media, Ecommerce, Communications, Digital Marketing, or Creative Strategy.
2. Senior individual contributors in those functions when they clearly influence creative or media buying.
3. Country or regional marketing leaders with direct responsibility for Argentina or Spain.

Accept Spanish or English title variants. Judge responsibilities, not exact wording.

Exclude CEO/founder/owner/general manager, unrelated C-suite, sales-only, partnerships-only, recruiting, HR, finance, legal, operations, engineering, interns, assistants, junior roles, consultants, freelancers, agency staff, former employees, and unverifiable roles.

## Country attribution

Use the country where the person/company has the relevant operating remit. Do not use nationality as a substitute. Count regional leaders only when Argentina or Spain is explicit and useful to the role.

## Verification hierarchy

Prefer evidence in this order:

1. Current LinkedIn profile/company page.
2. Current official company team, newsroom, or staff page.
3. Current first-party campaign/ad/social presence.
4. Two independent current sources that agree.

Search snippets are discovery aids, not sufficient final evidence when they conflict with a profile. Do not treat generated summaries, stale people databases, or guessed LinkedIn slugs as verified.

## Normalization for deduplication

- Lowercase and trim names and companies.
- Remove punctuation, legal suffixes, accents, and repeated whitespace for comparison only.
- Canonicalize LinkedIn URLs by removing query strings, fragments, locale prefixes, and trailing slashes.
- Treat renamed companies and parent/sub-brand relationships as possible duplicates and flag them.

## Evidence minimum

Every accepted candidate needs a direct profile URL, current employer/title, relevant country/remit, factual company-fit signal, factual role-fit explanation, and completed duplicate check. If one is unavailable, discard the candidate or report a shortage; never return it as actionable.
